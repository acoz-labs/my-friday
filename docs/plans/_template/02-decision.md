# Solution Decision

## Decision Drivers

List the issue-specific correctness, fit, security, operability, migration,
testability, cost, and simplicity criteria that decide among approaches.

## Competing Approaches

Develop materially distinct paths when the problem permits. Include a minimal
path and any credible architecture-improving or mechanism-level alternative.

## Adversarial Comparison

Attack each approach with verified repository and external evidence. Distinguish
fatal flaws, manageable tradeoffs, and unknowns that require validation.

## Selected Approach

State the chosen path, confidence, consequences, and why rejected mechanisms
lost. Preserve YAGNI boundaries and any product rescope recommendation.

## Decisions Ledger

Record consequential decisions in a compact table with rationale and evidence.
Do not turn ordinary implementation details into artificial architecture.
