# Context

## Problem And Desired Outcome

Restate the shaped product or operational problem in domain language. Link the
issue rather than copying its entire history.

## Current State

Map the relevant repository behavior, ownership paths, tests, documentation,
and deployment surfaces. Cite the source commit and exact paths or symbols.

## Actors And Critical Journeys

Identify affected users, operators, systems, and the success, denial, failure,
and recovery journeys that matter.

## Acceptance And Non-Goals

Check that the issue's criteria are designable. Record scope corrections and
explicit exclusions.

## Constraints, Dependencies, And Risks

Cover compatibility, data, permissions, secrets, external systems, migration,
operations, accessibility, and release concerns in proportion to risk.

## Evidence, Assumptions, And Unknowns

Separate verified facts from beliefs and unresolved evidence. Return to product
shaping only when the desired outcome cannot be designed without guessing.
